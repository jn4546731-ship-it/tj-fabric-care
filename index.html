Why Traditional Cleaners Are Broken

From estate mama fua scheduling headaches to corner dry cleaners losing garments or smelling of toxic chemicals. Here is how TJ Fabric Care changes the game.

Problem #1: Lost Garments

"Did they lose my favorite dress or suit?"

Old laundromats mix clothes from different homes or rely on paper tags that rip off in the wash.

TJ Solution

Garment-level RFID micro-barcodes. Every piece is scanned at your gate, washed separately, and insured up to KSh 50,000.

Problem #2: Toxic Solvents

"Why do dry cleaned suits smell of petrol?"

Traditional dry cleaners use Perchloroethylene (PERC) that irritates sensitive skin and triggers allergies.

TJ Solution

100% PERC-free ozone wet cleaning. Seaweed enzymes leave zero chemical scent—just fresh botanical fragrance.
